Straight answers

Know what you are buying before you install it.

Is AI Dent Guy a separate company from Snap Flow Solutions?

No. AI Dent Guy is Snap Flow Solutions’ PDR-specific product identity. Snap Flow Solutions is the company responsible for the offer, communication and any separately scoped services.

Is this a monthly subscription?

The snapshot is a $497 one-time purchase with no Snap Flow management retainer. You remain responsible for your own GoHighLevel plan and provider usage charges.

Does the snapshot include a working AI phone receptionist?

It includes a disabled PDR Voice AI template and setup/testing guidance. It does not include an AI subscription, phone number, usage billing, business configuration, activation or ongoing management.

Will it work in my existing GoHighLevel account?

It can, but a clean sub-account is recommended. Existing accounts may contain overlapping fields, workflows, calendars or pipelines that require a careful compatibility review before anything is published.

Is installation or customization included?

No. The base product is self-install. Installation, migration, cleanup, troubleshooting and custom workflows can be discussed separately through Snap Flow Solutions when availability allows.

Can I use it for more than one business?

The standard license is for one company and one GoHighLevel location. Unrelated businesses require separate licenses.

Does it automatically provide final dent estimates?

No. The intake and photo process helps organize preliminary review. Repairability and final pricing remain the responsibility of the repair business.
