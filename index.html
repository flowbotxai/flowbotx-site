The part nobody else does

We tell Google which ads actually made you money

Here’s what happens at most companies. Google Ads reports twelve leads last month. Nobody knows that four were wrong numbers, three wanted a service you don’t offer, and one turned into an $8,000 job. So Google keeps buying more of all twelve, because as far as it knows they’re identical.

We close that loop. Every click that reaches your site carries an ID. That ID follows the lead into the pipeline, through the estimate, to the job, to the invoice. When the job closes, the outcome and the dollar amount get sent back to Google Ads.

Now Google isn’t optimizing for form fills. It’s optimizing for customers who pay you. Same budget, aimed at the people who actually became jobs.

  5. 05Revenue is attached to the job
  6. 06The result goes back to Google

That’s also what makes the monthly report worth reading. Not clicks and impressions — cost per booked job, by source, with the revenue next to it.

Common questions

Straight answers

What exactly am I paying for every month?

Someone running your Google presence, answering and following up on every lead that comes in, asking your customers for reviews, and reporting on what it produced. It’s the work an office manager would do if you hired one, except it runs on systems and it doesn’t cost you a salary.

How do you know which ads actually made money?

Every click that reaches your site carries an ID. We keep that ID attached to the lead all the way through the pipeline to the closed job and the invoice, then send the outcome back to Google Ads. So instead of counting form fills, you get cost per booked job by source — and Google starts optimizing toward the customers who actually pay.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your number and use it exactly as you do now. We set up tracking behind it so we can tell which advertising produced which call.

Do you replace my scheduling software?

No. If you’re on Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan or similar, we sit in front of it and feed it. Your system stays your system.

What will the automated answering sound like to my customers?

Like a receptionist who knows your business. It answers, takes the details, books the appointment, and hands off to you when it should. We test it on your actual calls before it goes live, and you can listen to every one.

How long before I see anything?

Lead capture and answering work the day they’re switched on. Ad tracking starts producing useful numbers after a few weeks of jobs closing. Map pack and search movement is a three to six month build, not a switch. We’ll tell you which is which up front.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term contract. We ask for an initial term long enough to actually show results, then it’s month to month.

Who actually does the work?

We do. You’re not getting a login and a tutorial. We build it, we run it, and you deal with the owner directly.

What if I already have a website I like?

Then we don’t rebuild it. We audit it, fix what’s costing you leads, and put the capture and tracking behind it.

Do you only work with businesses in Metro Detroit?

That’s where we’re based and where most of our clients are. We work remotely too, but local is where we can be most useful.
